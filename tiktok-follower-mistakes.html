5 Mistakes That Stop Your TikTok Followers From Growing

Are your followers stuck? You might be making these 5 common mistakes.

1. Buying Fake Followers

Fake followers kill your reach. TikTok algorithm detects it.

2. Not Using Hashtags

Always use 3-5 relevant hashtags. Don't use #fyp only.

3. Low Video Quality

Use good light and clear audio. HD videos get more push.

4. Ignoring Trends

Follow trends but add your own twist.

5. Not Checking Analytics

Use TokLiveCounter and TikTok analytics to see what works.

Avoid these and your growth will be 2x faster!
