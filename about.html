About Us

Welcome to TokLiveCounter - toklivecounter.vercel.app

TokLiveCounter is a free tool designed to help TikTok creators, influencers, and fans track live follower counts in real-time. Our mission is to provide accurate, fast, and easy-to-use tools for the TikTok community.

We believe in transparency and helping creators grow. This website was created in 2026 to solve the problem of checking live followers without delay.
